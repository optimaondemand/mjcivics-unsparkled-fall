Setting: the 20th-Century American Home Front. Time: ~30 minutes. Category: Synchronous Homework and Activities.

This activity works whether you attend a live VR class session or complete it solo; the steps are identical either way. Enter the recreated Home Front. You will find three stations, each labeled with one draft era: Civil War (1863), World War II (1940s), and Vietnam (1960s), each displaying a posted placard summarizing that era's draft rules.

  1. At each of the three stations, leave a note recording the one detail of that era's draft rules you think made it more or less fair.
  2. Stand at the center table and use the whiteboard to rank the three drafts from most evenly shared burden to least evenly shared burden, based on your three notes.
  3. Record a short walkthrough of yourself narrating your ranking and defending your placement of the World War II draft specifically: do you agree with the lesson's claim that it was the most widely accepted of the three? Why or why not?
